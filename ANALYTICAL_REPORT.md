# TEMPLATE — Analytical Report Response

Use this template when responding to requests that require deep analysis, structured evaluation, or comprehensive examination of a topic.

**Trigger conditions:** "analyze", "evaluate", "assess", "deep dive", "examine", "break down", "investigate"

---

## Template

```markdown
## [Topic/Problem Statement]

### Executive Summary
[2–4 sentences. The most important finding or conclusion, stated upfront.
A busy reader who stops here should walk away with the core insight.]

---

### Context & Background
[1–2 paragraphs. What does the reader need to know to understand the analysis?
Define scope, key terms, and relevant background. Skip what's obvious to the audience.]

---

### Analysis

#### [Dimension 1 — e.g., Technical Factors]
[Examine this dimension. State what's observed, why it matters,
and what it implies. Be specific — avoid generic statements.]

**Key finding:** [One-sentence takeaway for this dimension]

#### [Dimension 2 — e.g., Risk Factors]
[Repeat structure]

**Key finding:** [One-sentence takeaway]

#### [Dimension 3 — e.g., Opportunities]
[Repeat structure]

**Key finding:** [One-sentence takeaway]

---

### Synthesis
[1–2 paragraphs. How do the dimensions interact? What does the combined
picture look like? This is where individual findings become a coherent story.
Don't just re-list findings — integrate them.]

---

### Limitations & Caveats
- [What assumptions does this analysis rest on?]
- [What information was unavailable or uncertain?]
- [Under what conditions might the conclusions change?]

---

### Recommendations / Conclusions
1. **[Action/Conclusion 1]** — [Brief rationale]
2. **[Action/Conclusion 2]** — [Brief rationale]
3. **[Action/Conclusion 3]** — [Brief rationale]

---

*Want me to go deeper on any specific dimension, or adjust the scope of this analysis?*
```

---

## Usage Notes

- Adapt the number and names of Analysis dimensions to fit the specific topic
- The Executive Summary goes **first** — never last
- Each dimension should have a "Key Finding" — forces precise conclusions
- Synthesis is the hardest and most valuable section — don't skip it
- Limitations are mandatory for any serious analysis
- Recommendations should be actionable, not platitudes

## What to Avoid

```
✗ "On one hand... on the other hand..." without conclusion
✗ Restating all dimensions in the Synthesis instead of integrating them
✗ Vague recommendations like "consider improving performance"
✗ Missing the Limitations section entirely
✗ Front-loading background at the expense of the actual analysis
```
